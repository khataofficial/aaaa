      Offline maps
      Pans/zooms to the area you need, then download. Tiles are saved on this device only. Zoom/pan on the map while online also caches what you view.
      Default is the public OpenStreetMap server, whose policy only allows small, polite downloads. For larger areas use your own/commercial tile provider here.
      Privacy & limits
      Your location stays on this device. It is never uploaded or stored on a server. Internet is used only for map tiles and (if you search) the place name you type. Tracking only runs after you press Start and stops when you press Stop.
      Platform limits: a web app cannot run a true background service. Keep this app open (screen stays awake while tracking). If the browser suspends the page, tracking pauses and the app warns you. For lock-screen tracking you need a native Android build.
